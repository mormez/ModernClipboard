⚡ Quick Snippets

Version 1.2 adds Quick Snippets — a folder pinned to the top of your snippets that holds up to 10 entries, each mapped to a number key.

  • Press ⇧⌘S and the Quick Snippets folder opens automatically.
  • Press 1–9 (or 0 for the 10th) to paste instantly — no arrow keys, no clicking.
  • Fill it with your most-used text under Edit Snippets. It starts with a few examples you can replace.

Prefer it not to auto-open? Turn it off in Preferences → General → Snippets.
